About me:
Graduate Electrical Engineer (Fresh) from Canadian University Dubai specializing in Mechatronics, Embedded Systems and Industrial Automation.

I enjoy making theory into reality in engineering. As part of my internship at Besomi Electronics, I ordered up an Arduino Opta PLC system, and wrote a Python-based real-time control bridge for a robotics simulation environment, and designed a cross-platform ROS 2 simulation environment (writing it up along the way so it can be handed off cleanly). In a year-long R&D internship with RQ Science & Technology, in collaboration with CUD, I participated in a space exploration rover project with the focus on fault-tolerant systems and precision control in a 5-DOF robotic arm.

Academically, I have created PLC-based automation systems (traffic light control, conveyor sorting, motor sequencing), designed sensor-driven navigation and sorting systems, and co-authored a published IEEE paper for impedance matching of piezoelectric smart-pavement systems.

I also gave Arduino/STEM workshops to 60+ students, was leading cross-functional technical projects with partners such as EGA and Al Ghurair Foundation, and have won two IEEE competitions in the process.

Experience: Software programming (Python, C++, Java) | Circuit Design & Signal Processing | PLC programming | SolidWorks | AI/ML basics

Someone who wants to share hands-on technical skills and a deep interest in addressing real engineering problems to a team that is tackling relevant energy and automation challenges.
